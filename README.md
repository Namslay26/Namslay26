### 🪴 A little about the human behind the commits

Hi, I'm **Sneha**! A software engineer by profession, a curious builder by nature, and someone who firmly believes that technology can be both useful *and* a little magical.

I spend my days working with software systems and my spare time wandering down interesting technical rabbit holes. Sometimes I emerge with a working application. Sometimes with twelve new ideas. Occasionally, both.

- 🛠️ **By day:** Building software with Java, Spring Boot, SQL, Kafka, and other pieces of the backend universe.
- 🔐 **Currently fascinated by:** Privacy engineering, compliance technology, and making data protection a little less painful.
- 🧠 **Into the rabbit hole of:** AI/ML, differential privacy, federated learning, and intelligent systems that solve actual problems.
- 🎨 **In my creative era:** Building little applications, experimenting with interfaces, and giving technical ideas a personality.
- 📚 **Forever learning:** Exploring new technologies, documenting what I discover, and figuring out how things work beneath the surface.
- 🌱 **Soft spot for:** Teaching, knowledge-sharing, collaborative projects, and helping people feel less intimidated by computer science.

> ✧ My personal philosophy: make complicated things understandable, useful things delightful, and learning a lifelong side quest.
